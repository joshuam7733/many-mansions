The 12 Pillars

The four wings of the house

Every pillar has two good sides. Your mix across all twelve is what makes your type.

Faith

  • DoctrineBy the Book ↔ Big Picture · How you relate to rules and counsel: exact obedience to the letter, or the spirit and context behind it.
  • TestimonyStudy & Reason ↔ Feeling & Prayer · Where your faith gets its fuel: deep study and evidence, or personal spiritual experience.
  • WorshipSacred Ritual ↔ Daily Service · Where you feel closest to God: temple and ordinances, or everyday acts of service.

Culture

  • RootsZion Core ↔ Mission Field · Whether you live surrounded by members (the Wasatch Front experience) or as a minority faith.
  • HeritagePioneer Stock ↔ Fresh Start · Multi-generation pioneer heritage, or first- and second-generation members writing a new story.
  • LifestyleCaffeine-Free ↔ Dirty Soda · How strictly you read the Word of Wisdom beyond the basics. No wrong answers, only drink orders.

Community

  • Ward LifeActivity Committee ↔ Back Row · Whether ward life energizes you, or you prefer to worship quietly and go home.
  • SharingEvery-Member Missionary ↔ Quiet Faith · How openly you talk about the gospel with friends, coworkers and online.
  • HomeBig Family ↔ Small & Simple · Your family-life ideal: a packed pew and huge reunions, or a smaller, simpler home.

Worldview

  • PoliticsConservative ↔ Progressive · Where you land on public policy. Members are found all across this line.
  • HistoryHistory Buff ↔ Present Focus · How much Church history (Nauvoo, Kirtland, the pioneers) shapes your faith today.
  • MediaConference Classic ↔ LDS Internet · Where your gospel content comes from: Conference and the Liahona, or podcasts and creators.

FAQ

Good questions

Why is it called Many Mansions?

It comes from John 14:2: "In my Father's house are many mansions." Latter-day Saints know the verse well (it is echoed in Doctrine and Covenants 98:18), and it captures the idea behind this test: the Church is full of very different people, and there is room for every one of them. Your type describes how you live your faith, never whether you belong.

Are the 16 types connected to the degrees of glory?

No. Joseph Smith taught that the many mansions include the kingdoms of glory, and that is exactly why no type here is higher or lower than another. All 16 types are in the same Father's house. We use the verse for its first promise to the disciples: there is a place prepared for each of us.

Are Mormons and Latter-day Saints the same people?

Yes. "Mormon" is a long-standing nickname for members of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, taken from the Book of Mormon. Since 2018 the Church has asked people to use its full name, and many members prefer "Latter-day Saints." This test is for anyone who identifies with either name.

Is this an official Church quiz?

No. Many Mansions is an independent, just-for-fun project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, and it does not represent Church doctrine.

Does this measure how righteous or faithful I am?

Not at all. Every pillar has two good sides. It describes how you live and express your faith: your culture, habits and style. It never ranks worthiness.

Can I take it if I am not a member?

Yes. Friends of the Church, investigators, former members and the simply curious are all welcome. Answer the way that fits your experience.

What happens to my answers?

Your answers stay in your own browser. Your shareable result link contains only your 12 pillar scores, nothing that identifies you.

How are the matches calculated?

Each type, scripture figure and mission has a profile across the same 12 pillars. We compare your scores with each profile and show the closest matches as a percentage.
